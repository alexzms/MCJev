# Minecraft facts (harness c3)

Things a Minecraft player knows. The c3 harness asks Jev which of these would help in the current situation (one
yes/no per fact, one forward pass) and shows the best few. Keep one fact per line, starting with "- ", written as
a player would say it. Unrelated facts are here on purpose: picking the right ones is part of the test.

- A creeper walks up to you silently and explodes about 1.5 seconds after it gets within 3 blocks of you; the blast hurts badly within about 5 blocks.
- A creeper's fuse stops if you get more than 7 blocks away from it or out of its sight before it explodes.
- Creepers cannot open doors: behind a closed door they cannot reach you, and the door blocks their sight so they do not explode.
- Creepers cannot climb ladders: 5 or more blocks above a creeper you are out of its reach.
- Creepers are afraid of cats and ocelots and keep about 6 blocks away from them.
- You open or close a door by looking at it within reach and right-clicking it.
- You climb a ladder by walking into it and holding forward; at the top keep going forward to step off.
- Sprinting is faster than a creeper or a zombie can walk.
- Zombies and skeletons burn in sunlight.
- Skeletons shoot arrows from a distance; hiding behind a wall blocks them.
- Spiders can climb walls.
- Endermen get angry if you look straight at them.
- Water puts out fire and slows mobs down.
- Iron golems protect villagers.
- Wolves can be tamed with bones.
- Eating food restores hunger, and with a full hunger bar health regenerates.
- Falling more than 3 blocks hurts.
- Redstone carries power from buttons and levers to doors and pistons.
- A bed lets you skip the night.
- Lava sets you on fire; do not walk into it.
- In sumo you win by knocking the other player off the ring and lose if you fall off; hits do no real damage there.
- In sumo, falling into the water under the ring kills you and you come back on the ring, but the round is lost.
- A hit while sprinting knocks the other player back much farther; after a sprint hit your sprint is spent, so let go of forward and press it again (w-tap) before the next hit.
- You can only hit a player within about 3 blocks, and a fist recharges in a quarter of a second.
- Moving sideways (strafing) while facing your opponent makes you harder to hit.
- Keep to the middle of a ring and never let yourself get between your opponent and the edge.
- A player with little ground behind them falls off when hit toward that edge.
- In Block UHC there is no natural regeneration: health you lose does not come back.
- A sword hit does full damage only once the sword has recharged, about 0.6 seconds after the last swing; switching items starts the recharge over.
- A hit while falling from a jump is a critical hit and does 50% more damage, but a critical hit cannot also be a sprint hit.
- A fully drawn bow (1 second) shoots an arrow hard and far; arrows drop over long distances, and blocks stop them.
- Lava sets a player on fire and slows them; the fire keeps burning after they leave the lava, and water puts it out.
- A water bucket poured at your feet puts out fire and breaks a fall; an empty bucket takes water or lava back.
- Pillaring up (jump and place a block under you) takes you out of reach of a sword.
- Since Minecraft 1.9 a fishing rod neither pulls nor knocks back other players.
